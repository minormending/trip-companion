Trip Companion

Paste an itinerary from anywhere. Places are located, the gaps between them are routed, and you get a briefing you can print or share. Everything runs in your browser, and signing in is optional.

Paste the trip’s link or its key. Use the view link from the Share menu, not the address bar you edit in.

Places and recorded facts from Photon, Overpass and OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Background from Wikipedia, CC BY-SA. Walking routes from OSRM. Light timings computed with the NOAA solar algorithm.
